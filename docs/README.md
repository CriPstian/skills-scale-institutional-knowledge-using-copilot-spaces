# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

## Overview

OctoAcme's project management processes are designed to ensure transparent, efficient, and consistent delivery across all cross-functional projects. This README provides a comprehensive overview of our framework, roles, execution practices, quality assurance standards, and continuous improvement approach.

## Project Initiation

The OctoAcme framework begins with a thorough initiation phase where new ideas undergo validation in collaboration with relevant stakeholders. This phase focuses on:

- **Clarifying the business need**: Understanding the problem we're solving and why it matters
- **Establishing measurable success criteria**: Defining clear metrics to evaluate project outcomes
- **Mapping out a lightweight plan**: Creating initial project structure and timeline

### Key Artifacts

- Project one-pager
- Stakeholder lists
- Risk registers

Once a project is approved, we move into the planning stage.

## Project Planning

The planning stage breaks down work into actionable increments and establishes the foundation for successful delivery:

- **Actionable increments**: Work is decomposed into manageable, deliverable pieces
- **Dependency mapping**: Identifying and documenting inter-team and technical dependencies
- **Formalized backlogs**: Prioritized work items with clear ownership
- **Timelines and quality expectations**: Structured through templates and checklists

## Roles and Responsibilities

Roles within OctoAcme are clearly delineated to foster accountability and smooth collaboration. Our comprehensive role definitions include:

### Core Roles
- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates overall delivery including schedules, risk management, and stakeholder communication
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines product direction, prioritizes the backlog, and defines success outcomes
- **Developers**: Build and maintain the product with high code quality standards and comprehensive test coverage
- **QA/Testing**: Validates quality by verifying deliverables meet acceptance criteria and conducting thorough testing

### Extended Roles
The following additional roles may be included based on project needs:

- **Project Sponsor**: Provides strategic alignment, secures funding, and removes organizational barriers
- **Business Analyst**: Clarifies requirements, facilitates stakeholder communication, and documents business needs
- **QA Lead**: Oversees test strategy, ensures quality standards, and manages defect processes
- **Change Manager**: Plans and monitors change management activities, stakeholder impact, and communications
- **Stakeholder Representatives**: Provide feedback and requirements from distinct user groups, attend regular reviews, and escalate issues

For detailed role descriptions, interactions, and a comprehensive role relationships summary, see [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md).

## Execution and Tracking

The execution phase is underpinned by defined team rhythms and communication cadences:

### Team Rhythms

- **Daily standups**: Surface blockers and coordinate next steps
- **Weekly delivery syncs**: Status updates and risk reviews
- **Sprint demos/reviews**: Showcase completed work at the end of each sprint

### Communication Structure

Communication is both structured and adaptable:

- **Structured communication**:
  - Weekly or milestone-based status updates
  - Risk escalation paths
  - Documented decision gates
  - Templates for regular status reporting
  - Incident management procedures

- **Adaptable approach**: Processes adjust to project needs while maintaining clear alignment among diverse teams and stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle

## Quality Assurance

Quality assurance is integral to every stage, not just at release. OctoAcme emphasizes:

### Automated Testing
- **Unit tests**: Validating individual components
- **Integration tests**: Ensuring components work together
- **End-to-end tests**: Verifying complete user workflows
- **Security scanning**: Integrated into continuous integration pipelines

### Manual QA
Complements automated coverage, especially for:
- Major feature deliveries
- Release candidates
- Complex user interactions

### Release Management

Each release follows a rigorously documented checklist to:
- Ensure readiness
- Minimize risk
- Provide mechanisms for rollback
- Enable rapid incident response

## Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement is embedded via retrospectives after every key milestone:

- **Surfacing learnings**: Identifying what worked well and what didn't
- **Tracking action items**: Ensuring follow-through on improvements
- **Driving iterative enhancements**: Small, incremental improvements to process and delivery
- **Team feedback**: Creating a culture of psychological safety and continuous learning
