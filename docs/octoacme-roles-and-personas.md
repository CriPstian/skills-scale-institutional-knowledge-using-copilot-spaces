# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide executive oversight and strategic alignment for projects. They secure funding, remove organizational barriers, and ensure the project aligns with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Secure and allocate project budget and resources
- Provide strategic direction and alignment with business goals
- Remove organizational and political barriers
- Make final go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Champion the project at executive and leadership levels
- Approve major scope changes and resource reallocations

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Align projects with organizational strategy
- Protect the project from external disruptions
- Enable project success through executive support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive briefings and milestone reviews
- Decision gates and budget approvals
- Escalation handling for high-priority blockers
- Strategic alignment sessions with Project Manager and Product Manager

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Provides status updates, escalates critical issues, seeks decisions on scope/budget changes
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on business value and strategic priorities
- **Steering Committee**: Represents project interests, advocates for resources
- **Stakeholder Representatives**: Receives consolidated feedback on business impact

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. They clarify requirements, document business needs, and facilitate effective stakeholder communication.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements
- Translate business needs into technical specifications
- Facilitate stakeholder workshops and requirements sessions
- Create process flows, use cases, and user stories
- Validate that solutions meet business needs
- Maintain requirements traceability matrix

### Goals
- Ensure clear, actionable requirements
- Bridge communication gaps between business and technical teams
- Minimize rework due to unclear requirements
- Maximize stakeholder satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Documentation of business processes and user stories
- Refinement sessions with Product Owner and developers
- Status updates on requirements clarity and changes

### Key Interactions
- **Product Owner/Product Manager**: Collaborates on backlog refinement and prioritization
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on scope, timelines, and requirements changes
- **Developers**: Clarifies requirements, answers questions during implementation
- **Stakeholder Representatives**: Gathers feedback, validates requirements
- **QA Lead**: Ensures test cases align with business requirements

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads oversee the quality assurance strategy and execution. They establish testing standards, manage defect processes, and ensure deliverables meet quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality standards
- Coordinate test planning and execution
- Manage defect tracking and resolution process
- Review test coverage and identify gaps
- Lead quality gates and release readiness assessments
- Mentor QA team members and promote quality best practices

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet acceptance criteria
- Minimize production defects and customer-impacting issues
- Establish efficient and effective testing processes
- Build a quality-first culture

### Typical Communication
- Weekly quality status updates
- Defect triage meetings with development team
- Test plan reviews with Product Owner
- Release readiness assessments

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Collaborates on defect resolution, reviews test results, shares quality metrics
- **Product Owner/Product Manager**: Validates acceptance criteria, prioritizes defect fixes
- **Project Manager**: Reports on quality metrics, highlights testing-related risks
- **Business Analyst**: Ensures test cases align with business requirements
- **Stakeholder Representatives**: Demonstrates quality during sprint reviews

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers plan and oversee change management activities, ensuring smooth adoption of new processes, tools, or organizational changes associated with project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change management strategy and plans
- Assess stakeholder impact and readiness
- Create communication plans for change rollout
- Coordinate training and adoption activities
- Monitor adoption metrics and resistance
- Facilitate feedback loops to address concerns

### Goals
- Maximize user adoption and minimize resistance
- Ensure stakeholders are prepared for changes
- Reduce disruption during transitions
- Sustain changes post-implementation

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and readiness reports
- Training schedules and adoption tracking
- Stakeholder communication and feedback sessions
- Post-implementation adoption reviews

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on timelines, coordinates change activities with project milestones
- **Team Leads**: Partners on training delivery and adoption monitoring
- **Stakeholder Representatives**: Assesses impact, gathers feedback, addresses concerns
- **Product Manager**: Ensures change strategy supports product adoption
- **Business Analyst**: Collaborates on process documentation and training materials

---

## Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives provide feedback and requirements from distinct user groups. They act as the voice of their constituencies, ensuring their needs are represented throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Represent the needs and interests of specific user groups
- Provide timely feedback on requirements and deliverables
- Attend regular reviews, demos, and planning sessions
- Escalate issues and concerns to appropriate parties
- Validate that deliverables meet user needs
- Communicate project updates to their constituencies

### Goals
- Ensure user needs are understood and addressed
- Facilitate effective two-way communication
- Identify potential issues early
- Support successful adoption within their user groups

### Typical Communication
- Sprint reviews and milestone demos
- Requirements validation sessions
- Feedback surveys and focus groups
- Regular sync meetings with Product Manager and Business Analyst

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Provides input on priorities, validates product direction
- **Business Analyst**: Participates in requirements gathering, provides user perspective
- **Project Manager**: Receives project updates, escalates concerns
- **Project Sponsor**: Provides constituency feedback to inform strategic decisions
- **Change Manager**: Shares adoption challenges, participates in training feedback

## Role Relationships Summary

The following table summarizes how OctoAcme roles interact and collaborate:

| Role | Primary Accountability | Key Partners | Provides To | Receives From |
|------|------------------------|--------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Project Sponsor** | Strategic alignment, funding, executive support | Project Manager, Product Manager, Steering Committee | Strategic direction, resources, executive decisions | Project status, escalations, ROI metrics |
| **Project Manager** | Delivery coordination, schedule, risks | All roles | Project plans, status updates, risk management | Requirements, blockers, team updates |
| **Product Manager** | Product vision, backlog prioritization | Business Analyst, Developers, Stakeholders | Product direction, priorities, acceptance criteria | User feedback, technical constraints, market insights |
| **Business Analyst** | Requirements clarity, stakeholder liaison | Product Manager, Developers, QA Lead, Stakeholders | Requirements, specifications, user stories | Business needs, feedback, technical questions |
| **Developers** | Implementation, code quality, testing | Product Manager, Project Manager, QA Lead | Completed features, technical insights, estimates | Requirements, priorities, test results |
| **QA Lead** | Quality strategy, testing standards, defect management | Developers, Product Manager, Business Analyst | Test plans, quality reports, defect status | Requirements, code changes, acceptance criteria |
| **Change Manager** | Change adoption, stakeholder readiness | Project Manager, Stakeholders, Team Leads | Change plans, training, adoption metrics | Impact feedback, resistance indicators, adoption data |
| **Stakeholder Representatives** | User group advocacy, feedback | Product Manager, Business Analyst, Change Manager | Requirements, feedback, validation | Product updates, training, communications |

## Project Proposal Checklist: Defining Roles and Personas

To ensure all project proposals clearly define involved roles and responsibilities, use this checklist during project initiation:

### Role Definition Checklist

- [ ] **Identify Core Roles**: List all roles required for project success (minimum: Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers)
- [ ] **Define Role Assignments**: Assign specific individuals to each role
- [ ] **Clarify Responsibilities**: Document key responsibilities for each role in the context of this project
- [ ] **Map Interactions**: Identify primary touchpoints and collaboration patterns between roles
- [ ] **Establish Authority Levels**: Define decision-making authority for each role (e.g., who approves scope changes, technical decisions)
- [ ] **Identify Hand-off Points**: Document key deliverable hand-offs between roles (e.g., requirements → development → testing)
- [ ] **Consider Extended Roles**: Determine if additional roles are needed:
  - [ ] Project Sponsor (for strategic oversight and executive support)
  - [ ] Business Analyst (for complex requirements or multiple stakeholder groups)
  - [ ] QA Lead (for dedicated quality oversight)
  - [ ] Change Manager (for significant organizational or process changes)
  - [ ] Stakeholder Representatives (for representing distinct user groups)
- [ ] **Document Communication Plan**: Define how and when roles will communicate
- [ ] **Review with Stakeholders**: Validate role definitions with key stakeholders
- [ ] **Update Project One-pager**: Include role definitions in project initiation documents

### Project One-pager Role Section Template

When completing the project one-pager, include:

```
## Team Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Name | Key Responsibilities | Decision Authority |
|------|------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Project Sponsor | [Name] | [2-3 key responsibilities] | [Approval authority level] |
| Project Manager | [Name] | [2-3 key responsibilities] | [Approval authority level] |
| Product Manager | [Name] | [2-3 key responsibilities] | [Approval authority level] |
| [Additional roles...] | [Name] | [2-3 key responsibilities] | [Approval authority level] |

## Key Hand-off Points
- [Role A] → [Role B]: [Deliverable/Activity]
- [Role B] → [Role C]: [Deliverable/Activity]
```
